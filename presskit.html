About Macdio

Macdio is a native internet radio player designed exclusively for the Apple ecosystem. It delivers a seamless listening experience across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch, and CarPlay — all from a single purchase with no subscriptions, no ads, and no tracking.

Built with 100% native SwiftUI, Macdio features real-time synced lyrics, live stream recording, a standalone Apple Watch app with LTE streaming, Dynamic Island integration, Family Sharing support, and effortless iCloud sync across all devices.

With access to over 55,000 radio stations worldwide and a lightweight ~5 MB footprint, Macdio proves that a powerful app doesn't need to be bloated.

Brand Guidelines

Please follow these guidelines when using Macdio brand assets.

Do's & Don'ts

  • ✅ Use the official logos and icons provided above without modification.
  • ✅ Maintain adequate spacing around the logo — at least half the icon's width on all sides.
  • ✅ Use the dark background version on dark surfaces, and the light version on light surfaces.
  • ❌ Do not stretch, rotate, or distort the logo.
  • ❌ Do not change the logo colors or add effects (shadows, outlines, etc.).
  • ❌ Do not use the Macdio name or logo to imply endorsement without permission.
  • ❌ Do not crop the app icon — always show the full rounded-rectangle shape.
