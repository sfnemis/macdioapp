Privacy Policy — tvOS

Last updated: May 1, 2026

📱 iOS 🖥 macOS 📺 tvOS ⌚ watchOS

📋 Overview

Macdio for Apple TV is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

🔍 Data Collection

Macdio does not collect any personal information. All your data stays on your Apple TV and syncs only through your personal iCloud account.

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

📊 Usage Data (Privacy Nutrition Label)

Macdio collects Other Usage Data for App Functionality. This refers to your listening history — which stations you played, for how long, and on which device.

This data is:

  • Not linked to your identity
  • Not used for tracking
  • Stored only in your private iCloud container
  • Used solely to display your personal listening statistics across devices

📺 tvOS-Specific

Macdio on Apple TV does not support recording. The app is streaming-only and does not store any audio files on your device. Top Shelf data is processed locally to display your recently played stations.

🌐 Third-Party Services

Macdio connects to the following services for core functionality:

  • Radio Browser API — to browse stations. No personal data sent.
  • LRCLIB — to fetch lyrics. No personal data sent.
  • iTunes Search API — to fetch artwork. No personal data sent.

☁️ iCloud Sync

Your favorites, listening history, and settings sync via your private iCloud container managed by Apple.

Refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.
