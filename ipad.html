Frequently asked questions

Is the iPad app included?
Yes. One purchase covers iPad, iPhone, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch, with Family Sharing.
Does it sync with my other devices?
Favorites, folders and saved songs sync through iCloud automatically. See Macdio for iPhone, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch.
Can I record on iPad?
Yes, including Split by Track which turns a session into individual named songs. See the recording guide.
Does song identification use the microphone?
Never. Macdio requests no microphone permission at all and identifies songs from the stream itself.
