Comparison

Looking for a TuneIn alternative?

I make one of the apps on this page, so read it that way. I have still tried to write the version I would want to read before paying for anything.

What TuneIn does better

TuneIn runs on Android, in a browser, on Sonos, on Alexa and on Google speakers. Macdio runs on Apple hardware and nothing else. If half your listening happens on a smart speaker or an Android phone, stop reading here. TuneIn is the right answer and no comparison table changes that.

TuneIn also carries live sport and news feeds it licenses directly. Macdio has no licensing deals with anyone, it plays public station streams, so a game that only exists on a TuneIn feed is not in Macdio at all.

And TuneIn has a free tier. Macdio does not. There is no trial and no free version, you pay once or you do not use it.

What people usually mean when they search for this

From the mail I get, the same three things come up. Ads before and during a stream that the station itself never broadcast. A subscription appearing in front of stations that used to be free. An account you have to create before the app will remember a favourite.

Macdio answers those three the same way every time: no ads, one purchase instead of a subscription, and no account beyond the Apple ID you already have. Favourites sync through your own iCloud, so nothing about you sits on a server I run, because I do not run one.

The catalogue

Macdio reads its station list from Radio Browser, a community-run open database, and that is where the 55,000 figure comes from. It is not curated and it is not mine. A station can go silent and nobody updates the entry, the same as every other directory. If a station you need is missing you can paste its stream address in by hand, and there is a scan that pulls the stream out of a station website. The guide to finding a stream URL covers how to get that address.

Moving over

There is no import. TuneIn offers no favourites export and I am not going to pretend a scraper counts as a feature. What people do in practice is search the ten or fifteen stations they actually listen to, heart them once, and let iCloud copy them to every device they own. It takes a few minutes.

What Macdio does that TuneIn does not

It records a live stream straight from the network and splits the session into one file per song, naming each track on its own. Songs you love land in your Apple Music library. Lyrics scroll in time with what is playing. Song identification reads the stream itself, so it works with the phone in your pocket and Macdio never asks for microphone permission, which is covered in the guide on naming a song from the radio. The Apple Watch app streams over LTE with no iPhone nearby.

One limit worth stating plainly: recordings stay inside the app. There is no export, no sharing and no copy into Files. I decided that on purpose and it is not going to change.

So which one

Pick TuneIn if you listen on more than Apple hardware, if you want live sport, or if you are not going to pay for a radio app. Pick Macdio if everything you own has an Apple logo on it, if ads are the reason you are reading this page, and if recording or lyrics sound useful to you. I would rather you pick right the first time than refund later.

Common questions

Is Macdio free like TuneIn?
No. It is a single purchase, 9.99 US dollars in the United States and priced locally elsewhere, with no subscription, no ads and no free tier. That one purchase covers iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay.
Can I import my TuneIn favourites?
No. TuneIn has no favourites export, so stations have to be added again by hand. Most people re-add the handful they actually listen to and let iCloud copy them to their other devices.
Does Macdio have the same stations as TuneIn?
Mostly, but not exactly. Macdio reads the Radio Browser open database. Anything TuneIn licenses directly, such as live sport, is not in there. A missing station can be added by pasting its stream address.
Is there an Android or web version?
No, and there is no plan for one. Macdio is written for Apple platforms only.
Does Macdio show ads or track me?
No ads, no analytics inside the app, and no account. Favourites and history sync through your own iCloud, so the data stays with Apple rather than with me.
