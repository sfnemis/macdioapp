Pricing

Pay once. Listen everywhere.

One purchase, every Apple device you own, every update since launch. No subscription, no ads, no account, and nothing unlocked later for more money.

As of 12 October 2026 Macdio is a one-time purchase: 14.99 US dollars in the United States, priced in local currency in 175 App Store territories. That single purchase covers iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay through Apple's Universal Purchase, so buying it once on the phone puts it on the Mac and the TV too. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase, no ads and no account to create.

What the price includes

The app on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay, 60,000+ radio stations, 4.7 million podcasts, recording, Song ID, synced lyrics, on-device transcripts and highlights, and every update. 1.0 to 3.0 cost existing owners nothing.

What it does not include

Android, Windows or a web player: Macdio is written for Apple platforms only. There is no free tier and no trial; Apple's refund process applies if it is not for you.

Why there is no subscription

Radio has been free on the air for a century. A monthly fee for something that should just work never made sense to me, so the price is the price, and the stations stay yours.

Local prices

Apple sets a price tier per country, so a listener in India or Brazil pays the price that fits that market, not a US dollar conversion. The home page shows the price for the country your Mac or iPhone is set to, and the App Store shows the final figure before you buy.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too: Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com handles them, usually within a few days. I never see your payment details, which is also why there is no account.

Updates

Every update since 1.0 has been free for existing owners, including the 3.0 release that added podcasts, transcripts and highlights. A paid upgrade is not planned. If that ever changes it will be a new app, not a lock on the one you bought.

Common questions

Is there a free version or a trial?
No. Macdio is a single purchase with every feature included. If it turns out not to be for you, Apple's refund process applies.
Do I pay again on the Mac or the Apple TV?
No. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay through Universal Purchase. Sign in with the same Apple ID and install it.
Will the next big version cost extra?
Every update so far has been free, including the 3.0 podcast release, and a paid upgrade is not planned.
Why is the price different in my country?
Apple prices each country on its own tier. The figure the App Store shows you is the one you pay.
Is there an Android or web version?
No, and none is planned. Macdio is written for Apple platforms only.
