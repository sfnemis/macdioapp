Comparison

Looking for a myTuner alternative?

No ads, no account to sync your favorites, a Mac app that is actually a Mac app, and one purchase instead of nine in-app products. Macdio plays 60,000+ stations on every Apple device you own.

As of 12 October 2026 myTuner Radio is free with visual ads, needs an account to sync favorites, and lists subscriptions such as 9.99 US dollars a month or 34.99 a year, plus a 79.99 "Radio Pro" product; on the Mac it runs as the iPad app on Apple silicon, or as myTuner Radio Pro, a separate 9.99 US dollar Mac app last updated in 2018. Macdio is 14.99 US dollars once, has no ads and no account, syncs through your own iCloud, and runs as a native app on the Mac next to iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay.

What people usually mean when they search for this

Ads before a stream the station never broadcast. A sign-up wall in front of the favorites list. A price list that is hard to read. Macdio answers each the same way: no ads, no account, one price, every Apple device.

Native on the Mac

A real Mac app with a menu bar player, keyboard shortcuts and Dock artwork, not a phone app stretched onto a desktop window.

Song ID without a microphone

Stations that send no titles get the song name and cover from the stream itself; the app has no microphone permission at all. How it works

Recording, lyrics, podcasts

Record a stream into one file per song, read synced lyrics, and follow 4.7 million podcasts with transcripts made on your device.

What myTuner does that Macdio does not

A free tier, Android and a web player: myTuner is everywhere and costs nothing to try. An equalizer: Macdio has no audio effects. A larger podcast catalog is not one of them; both read the open Podcast Index, and myTuner states 1 million podcasts to Macdio's 4.7 million. If free or Android is the point, myTuner stays the right choice.

Moving over

myTuner has no favorites export, so stations are added again by hand: search the ten or fifteen you actually listen to, heart them once, and iCloud copies them to every device. A station that is missing can be added by pasting its stream address.

One thing to know before you switch

Macdio is built for Apple devices only. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay are covered by one purchase, and there is no Android or web version. If part of your listening happens somewhere else, this is not the app for you.

Common questions

Can I import my myTuner favorites?
No. myTuner has no export. Most people re-add the handful they listen to and let iCloud copy them to their other devices.
Does Macdio have the same stations?
Macdio reads the Radio Browser open database, 60,000+ stations, plus its own copy when that database is down. A missing station can be added from its stream address.
Is Macdio free?
No. It is a one-time purchase of 14.99 US dollars in the United States, priced locally elsewhere, with no ads and no subscription.
Does Macdio have an alarm?
Yes, on iPhone and iPad: wake to a station or to the newest episode of a show, once, daily or on weekdays.
Is there an Android version?
No, and none is planned.
