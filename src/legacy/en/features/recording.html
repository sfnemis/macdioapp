Feature

Record the radio. Get back a library.

Capture any live stream from the network, with no microphone and no room noise. Split by track gives you one file per song, each named, and the songs inside every recording are listed.

As of 12 October 2026 Macdio records live radio on iPhone, iPad and Mac. The recording is taken from the stream itself, so there is no microphone and no ambient noise. Split by Track cuts the session into one file per song using the station's own song boundaries, with a minimum-length guard so a corrected title never cuts a song in two. The Song ID engine then scans the finished recording and lists the songs in it with timestamps. Formats are the original stream, MP3, AAC or M4A at the original quality or 128, 192 or 256 kbps.

How a session becomes tracks

Press record while a station plays. Macdio writes the stream to a file and watches the station's metadata for the moment one song ends and the next begins. With Split by Track on, each boundary closes one file and opens the next, named after the song. Stations that send no titles are recorded as one file, and Song ID names the songs inside it afterwards.

Limits, stated plainly

  • Recording works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Apple TV and Apple Watch play but do not record.
  • A recording stops on its own after three hours by default; you can change the maximum length. There is no scheduled recording at a set time.
  • Recording refuses to start with less than 500 MB free, so it never fills the device.
  • Recordings stay inside the app. There is no export, share sheet, AirDrop or copy into Files; that was a decision made with App Review and it will not change.

Formats and quality

Original keeps the stream exactly as broadcast, which for an AAC or MP3 station is the best possible copy. MP3, AAC and M4A re-encode at 128, 192 or 256 kbps when you want smaller files or a format a particular player prefers. FLAC and Ogg streams are kept as they are in Original.

Is it allowed?

Recording a broadcast for your own listening is the same thing a cassette deck did for decades, and in most countries personal use is fine. Sharing or selling a recording is not, which is also why Macdio keeps recordings inside the app. The recording guide goes through the law country by country.

Common questions

Can Macdio record internet radio?
Yes, on iPhone, iPad and Mac, straight from the stream with no microphone. Split by Track makes one named file per song.
Can I schedule a recording?
No. Recording starts by hand and stops after the maximum length you set, three hours by default.
Can I export or share a recording?
No. Recordings stay in the app, by design and by agreement with App Review.
Which formats does it record in?
The original stream, MP3, AAC or M4A, at the original quality or 128, 192 or 256 kbps.
Does it work on Apple TV or Apple Watch?
No. Those versions play radio but do not record.
