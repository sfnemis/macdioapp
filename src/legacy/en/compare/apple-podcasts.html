Comparison

Macdio and Apple Podcasts, side by side.

Apple Podcasts comes with every Apple device and costs nothing. Here is what a paid app has to add to earn its place next to it, and where Apple still wins.

As of 12 October 2026 Apple Podcasts is free, built into iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch, syncs through your Apple ID, and shows transcripts that Apple provides for supported shows and languages, with a share button for a moment from the transcript. Macdio is 14.99 US dollars once: it makes a transcript for any episode on your own device in a language the device supports, searches across every transcript, sends a marked moment with its sentence to Apple Notes, Notion, Obsidian, Bear, Craft or Readwise, lists the songs an episode plays, and keeps 60,000+ live radio stations in the same app.

Where Apple Podcasts wins

Price, obviously. Video podcasts, channel subscriptions, and the catalog and reviews that come with being the default. Transcripts for the shows Apple covers appear without any work on your device. If you follow a handful of big shows and never need the words outside the app, there is no reason to pay for anything.

Where Macdio wins

Any episode, any show

Macdio's transcript is made on the device from the audio, so a small show in a supported language gets one too, not only the shows a catalog has processed. How it works

The words leave the app

A highlight is the second plus the sentence, sent to the notes app you already use. Apple Podcasts shares a moment; it does not file it. How it works

Radio and podcasts together

60,000+ stations, Song ID, recording and synced lyrics in the same app, and a queue that runs an episode, then a station, then the next episode.

Using both

Many people do. Apple Podcasts has no OPML export, so shows are added to Macdio by search or from another app's OPML; after that the two apps do not need to know about each other. Macdio is the one to open when you want the words, the notes or the radio.

Common questions

Is Macdio better than Apple Podcasts?
For transcripts of any episode, highlights into your notes and live radio, yes. For price, video podcasts and channel subscriptions, Apple Podcasts wins. The table above puts the two side by side.
Does Apple Podcasts have transcripts?
Yes, provided by Apple for supported shows and languages, with a share button for a moment. Macdio makes its own on your device for any episode in a supported language.
Can I move my Apple Podcasts subscriptions to Macdio?
Apple Podcasts has no OPML export, so shows are added by search in Macdio, or through an OPML file from another app you may have used.
Does Macdio need an account?
No. Favorites, shows, transcripts and highlights sync through your own iCloud.
How much does Macdio cost?
14.99 US dollars once in the United States, priced locally elsewhere, for every Apple device together.
