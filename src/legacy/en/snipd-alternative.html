Highlights with the spoken sentence, sent to Apple Notes, Obsidian, Notion or Readwise, from an app that also runs on the iPad, the Mac and the TV. Transcripts made on your device, one purchase, no Premium.

As of 12 October 2026 Snipd runs on the iPhone and Apple Watch, makes its transcripts and AI summaries on its servers, and sells Premium at 6.99 US dollars a month for the AI features; its exports go to Notion, Readwise, Obsidian, Glasp, Bear, Logseq and Markdown. Macdio runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay, makes transcripts on the device with nothing uploaded, sends highlights to Apple Notes, Notion, Obsidian, Bear, Craft and Readwise, and costs 14.99 US dollars once.

What Snipd does that Macdio does not

Snipd's AI suggests highlights on its own and writes notes for you; Macdio marks only what you mark, and its summaries and chapters come from Apple's language model, on the device or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Snipd exports to Glasp and Logseq and as Markdown files; Macdio sends to six apps and to the clipboard. Snipd has a free plan to try; Macdio is a purchase. If automatic AI snips are the reason you use Snipd, it keeps that edge.

Moving over

Export an OPML file from Snipd and open it in Macdio; your shows come across. Existing snips stay in the notes apps you already sent them to, which is the point of sending them there.

One thing to know before you switch

Macdio is built for Apple devices only. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay are covered by one purchase, and there is no Android or web version. If part of your listening happens somewhere else, this is not the app for you.

Common questions

Does Macdio run on the iPad and the Mac?
Yes, as native apps, plus Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay, all in one purchase.
Can Macdio send highlights to Apple Notes?
Yes, through a shortcut you add once on iPhone and iPad and through AppleScript on the Mac. Notion, Obsidian, Bear, Craft and Readwise are the other destinations.
Does Macdio suggest highlights with AI?
No. You mark the moments; Macdio saves the sentence and sends it where you want it.
Are the transcripts made in the cloud?
No. Apple's speech recognition runs on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and the audio is not uploaded.
Is there a free plan?
No. Macdio is 14.99 US dollars once in the United States, priced locally elsewhere.
