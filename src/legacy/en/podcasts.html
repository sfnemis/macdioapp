Podcasts

Podcasts, with the words on your side.

Follow any of 4.7 million podcasts and play episodes next to your stations. Every episode becomes searchable text on your own device, with chapters, a summary, the songs it plays, and a highlight button that sends a moment to your notes.

As of 12 October 2026 Macdio searches the 4.7 million podcasts of the Podcast Index and plays them in the same app as 60,000+ radio stations, on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay. Transcripts are made on the iPhone, iPad or Mac you listen on by Apple's speech recognition; the audio is never uploaded and there is no account. The whole podcast side is part of the one-time purchase, with no subscription.

Transcripts made on your device

Apple's speech recognition turns each episode into text on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, in the episode's language where the device supports it. Nothing leaves the device. How transcripts work

Search inside every episode

Type a word and Macdio finds the sentences that contain it across every transcript in your library, then jumps to that second.

Chapters and a summary

Publisher chapters when the show provides them, generated chapters and a written summary when it does not, made from the transcript.

Songs in the episode

The songs a music podcast plays are identified from the audio and listed with timestamps, and saved to Tracks when you heart them.

Highlights to your notes

Mark a moment with a button, with Siri or from the watch. The second and the sentence go to Apple Notes, Notion, Obsidian, Bear, Craft or Readwise. How highlights work

Up Next with live radio

Queue episodes and a station together. A station plays for the time you give it, then hands over to the next episode.

What else the podcast side does

  • Episode downloads for listening offline, and a show's repeated segments learned from two downloaded episodes so they can be skipped.
  • OPML import and export, so subscriptions move in from Overcast, Pocket Casts or any app that exports OPML, and out again whenever you want.
  • New episode alerts on the iPhone for the shows you follow.
  • Charts of the popular shows in your country, and a search across the whole Podcast Index.
  • Playback speed per episode; Macdio has no effects such as trimmed silence or volume boost.
  • Everything synced through your own iCloud: shows, episodes, transcripts, highlights and progress, with no account.

On each device

Transcripts, chapters, summaries, song lists and highlight export are made on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Apple TV plays episodes and shows the transcripts, chapters and songs that synced from your other devices through iCloud. Apple Watch plays podcasts on its own Wi-Fi or cellular connection, with no iPhone nearby, and can mark a moment. CarPlay has a Podcasts tab with Continue Listening, your shows and their episodes.

Common questions

Does Macdio play podcasts?
Yes, since version 3.0. Follow any of the 4.7 million podcasts in the Podcast Index, play episodes next to your radio stations, and queue both in Up Next.
Are the transcripts really made on the device?
Yes. Apple's speech recognition runs on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and the audio is not uploaded. Apple TV shows transcripts that synced from those devices.
Which languages do transcripts work in?
The episode's own language, where your device supports it for on-device recognition. The list depends on the device and the OS version; the app shows which languages are available.
Can I move my subscriptions from another app?
Yes, with an OPML file. Overcast, Pocket Casts and most other podcast apps export one (Apple Podcasts does not, so its shows are added by search); Macdio imports every show in it and exports your list the same way.
Does the podcast side cost extra?
No. It is part of the one purchase, with no subscription.
Can I listen offline?
Yes. Episodes download to the device for playback without a connection.
