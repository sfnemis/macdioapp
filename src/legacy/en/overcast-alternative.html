Comparison

Looking for an Overcast alternative?

A native Mac app and an Apple TV app, transcripts for every episode made on your device, highlights to Apple Notes or Obsidian, and live radio in the same queue. One purchase, no ads promoting other shows.

As of 12 October 2026 Overcast is free and funded by ads that promote other podcasts, with Premium at 19.99 US dollars a year to hide them; it runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, its Mac entry is the iPad app on Apple silicon, and there is no Apple TV app. Macdio is 14.99 US dollars once with no ads, runs natively on the Mac and on Apple TV as well as iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and CarPlay, makes transcripts on the device, sends highlights to six notes apps, and plays 60,000+ radio stations next to the episodes.

What people usually want when they search for this

The Mac. Overcast listeners who work at a desk keep asking for a real Mac app, and the TV comes up for the living room. Then transcripts without a wait, and notes out of the player. Macdio has all four; what it lacks is Overcast's audio engineering, and that is stated below.

Mac and Apple TV, native

The Mac app has a menu bar player and keyboard shortcuts; the Apple TV app has Continue Listening and Latest Episodes on the big screen, with transcripts synced from your other devices.

Transcripts and highlights

Every episode becomes text on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, searchable, with chapters and a summary; a marked moment goes to Apple Notes, Notion, Obsidian, Bear, Craft or Readwise.

Radio in the queue

Up Next plays an episode, then a live station for the time you give it, then the next episode. 60,000+ stations, Song ID, recording and lyrics come with it.

What Overcast does that Macdio does not

Smart Speed and Voice Boost: Overcast shortens silences and evens out quiet voices, and it does both very well. Macdio has playback speed and no effects. Overcast is also free to use. If Smart Speed is the reason you stay, that is a good reason.

Moving over

Overcast exports an OPML file from its settings; open it in Macdio and every show comes across. Playback positions do not travel between podcast apps, so the newest episodes start fresh.

One thing to know before you switch

Macdio is built for Apple devices only. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay are covered by one purchase, and there is no Android or web version. If part of your listening happens somewhere else, this is not the app for you.

Common questions

Does Macdio have a real Mac app?
Yes, a native Mac app with a menu bar player, keyboard shortcuts and Dock artwork, not the iPad app in a window.
Does Macdio have Smart Speed or Voice Boost?
No. Playback speed is the only audio control. Overcast keeps the edge on audio effects.
Can I import my Overcast subscriptions?
Yes, with the OPML file Overcast exports. Shows come across; playback positions do not.
Does Macdio show ads?
No ads of any kind, and no account. It is a one-time purchase.
How much does Macdio cost?
14.99 US dollars once in the United States, priced locally elsewhere, for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay together.
