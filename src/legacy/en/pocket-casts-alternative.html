Looking for a Pocket Casts alternative?

Transcripts for every episode made on your own device, highlights sent to Apple Notes or Obsidian, live radio in the same app, and no Plus tier. One purchase, no ads, no account.

As of 12 October 2026 Pocket Casts is free with advertising and sells Plus at 3.99 US dollars a month or 39.99 a year, with Patron above that; transcripts and AI chapters are Plus features and sync needs an account. Macdio is 14.99 US dollars once: transcripts for any episode are made on the device, highlights go to six notes apps, favorites and progress sync through your own iCloud with no account, and 60,000+ radio stations live in the same app.

What Pocket Casts does that Macdio does not

Android, the web player and Windows: Pocket Casts runs almost everywhere, Macdio runs on Apple devices only. Audio effects: Pocket Casts has trim silence and volume boost; Macdio has playback speed and nothing else. Folders and filters: Pocket Casts has deep organization tools; Macdio keeps shows, episodes, Up Next and Highlights. If those matter to you, Pocket Casts is the better fit and I would rather say so here than in a refund.

Moving over

Export an OPML file from Pocket Casts (Settings, Export Podcasts) and open it in Macdio. Every show comes across; episode progress and your bookmarks do not, since no app exports those. Transcripts are made fresh on your device as you play.

One thing to know before you switch

Macdio is built for Apple devices only. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay are covered by one purchase, and there is no Android or web version. If part of your listening happens somewhere else, this is not the app for you.

Common questions

Do I need a subscription for transcripts in Macdio?
No. Transcripts are part of the one purchase and are made on your device for any episode in a supported language.
Can I import my Pocket Casts subscriptions?
Yes, with the OPML file Pocket Casts exports. Shows come across; progress and bookmarks do not.
Does Macdio have Trim Silence or Volume Boost?
No. Playback speed is the only audio control. If trimmed silence matters to you, Pocket Casts keeps the edge there.
Is there an Android or web version of Macdio?
No, and none is planned. Macdio is written for Apple platforms only.
How much does Macdio cost?
14.99 US dollars once in the United States, priced locally elsewhere, for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay together.
