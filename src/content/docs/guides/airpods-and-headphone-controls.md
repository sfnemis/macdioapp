In Macdio a single press on AirPods or a headphone button pauses and plays. On live radio a double press plays the next station and a triple press the previous one. During a podcast episode a double press skips forward 30 seconds and a triple press skips back 15 seconds, unless you turn off "Headphone Presses Skip". This works on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and the same commands drive the Lock Screen, Control Center, CarPlay and the Mac's media keys.

## Quick steps

To choose what AirPods do during a podcast episode on iPhone or iPad:

1. Open Settings in Macdio. On iPhone it sits under More unless you moved it into the tab bar.
2. Scroll to the "Podcasts" section.
3. Leave "Headphone Presses Skip" on to skip inside the episode, or turn it off to move between episodes.
4. Set "Skip Back" and "Skip Forward" to the jumps you want.

The change applies at once, even to the episode that is playing.

## What each press does

| Press | Live radio | Episode, "Headphone Presses Skip" on | Episode, setting off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single | Pause or play | Pause or play | Pause or play |
| Double | Next station in your list | Skip forward (30 s by default) | Next episode, from Up Next first, then the show's list |
| Triple | Previous station in your list | Skip back (15 s by default) | Restart the episode, or the previous episode if you are in its first 3 seconds |

"Headphone Presses Skip" is on by default. It only changes podcast episodes, never live radio.

When you play a recording, a double or triple press moves to the next or previous recording. Inside a continuous recording it jumps to the next or previous song Macdio detected. [Recording in Macdio](/guides/recording-in-macdio.html) explains both kinds.

## Which list do next and previous station use?

Next and previous move through the list you started the station from: Favorites, one of your lists, or search results, for example. They stop at the ends of that list and do not wrap around to the first station. If there is no list, a double press does nothing.

The simplest way to get a useful list is to start a station from Favorites. Saying "Play my favorites on Macdio" to Siri does the same, see the [Siri guide](/guides/siri-radio.html). How favorites and lists are kept in order is in [favorites, lists and sync](/guides/favorites-lists-and-sync.html).

## How does pause work on live radio?

By default, pausing a station mutes it and keeps the stream open for 60 seconds. Press play within that minute and the sound comes back at once, at the live point. After 60 seconds Macdio closes the stream, and play connects to the station again.

Turn on "Live Pause" if you want a real pause instead. Pause then holds your place, play continues where you stopped, and tapping the time in the LIVE badge takes you back live. It is off by default.

| Platform | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| iPhone and iPad | Settings, "Playback" section, "Live Pause" |
| Mac | Settings window, "General" tab, "Playback" section, "Live Pause" |
| Apple TV | Settings, "Audio" card, "Live Pause" |

A pause that comes from a phone call or Siri does not count as your pause, so the radio can come back after the call. A play press while a station is still connecting is ignored, and the connection carries on. And while a game controller is connected, pause always mutes for up to 60 seconds, even with Live Pause on; the [game controller guide](/guides/game-controllers.html) has the reason.

## Where are the headphone settings on the Mac?

On the Mac the same options sit in the Settings window, "Podcasts" tab, "Playback" section, with sentence case labels:

| Setting | iPhone and iPad | Mac | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Presses skip | "Headphone Presses Skip" | "Headphone presses skip" | On or off | On |
| Back jump | "Skip Back" | "Skip back" | 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60 s | 15 s |
| Forward jump | "Skip Forward" | "Skip forward" | 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 s | 30 s |

The skip intervals are also the ones the Lock Screen and CarPlay skip buttons use. Apple TV and Apple Watch have no switch for this, and the default (on) applies there.

## What do the Lock Screen and Control Center show?

For a live station you get previous and next station arrows and no scrubber. The top line shows the song, or the station name when the station sends no title, and the second line shows the station. While a station connects, the second line reads "Connecting…".

For a podcast episode the arrows are replaced by skip back and skip forward buttons with your intervals, and a scrubber appears. Macdio also offers the system a speed control from 0.8x to 3.0x. A speed change made from the Lock Screen, CarPlay or Siri applies to the episode.

## Do the Mac media keys work?

Yes. The play/pause key and the next and previous keys on a Mac keyboard send the same commands as headphones, so everything in the table above applies: next station on the radio, skips or next episode during a podcast.

## Does it work in the car?

Yes. The Now Playing buttons in CarPlay and the skip buttons on a steering wheel use the same commands. The [CarPlay guide](/guides/carplay-radio.html) covers the rest of the car screen.

## Limits worth knowing

- Next and previous station stop at the end of the list instead of starting over. A game controller cycles round instead.
- "Headphone Presses Skip" has no effect on live radio.
- Which gesture on your AirPods sends which command is set in the AirPods settings of iOS, not in Macdio.
- On Apple TV the Siri Remote controls playback, and there is no headphone setting in the TV app.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does a double press on AirPods do in Macdio?

On live radio it plays the next station in the list you started from. During a podcast episode it skips forward, 30 seconds by default.

### How do I make AirPods go to the next podcast episode instead of skipping 30 seconds?

Turn off "Headphone Presses Skip" in Macdio's Settings, in the "Podcasts" section. On the Mac it is "Headphone presses skip" in the "Podcasts" tab of the Settings window.

### Why does next station do nothing on my AirPods?

Next and previous move only inside the list you started the station from, and they stop at the end of that list. Start the station from Favorites or one of your lists first.

### Does pausing live radio resume where I stopped?

Only with "Live Pause" on. By default a pause mutes the stream, and pressing play within 60 seconds brings you back at the live point.

### Do the Mac keyboard media keys work with Macdio?

Yes. Play/pause and the next and previous keys use the same commands as headphones, so they behave the same way.

### Why does the Lock Screen show skip buttons instead of next and previous?

During a podcast episode Macdio shows skip back and skip forward buttons with your own intervals, plus a scrubber. Live radio shows previous and next station instead.
