Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my own radio station?

Open the app, click the "+" button, paste any stream URL (e.g. .m3u, .pls, or direct stream URL), give it a name, and you're live in seconds.

How do I record a live stream?

While listening to a station, click the record button in the player. Macdio saves the audio in high quality. Find all your recordings in the Recordings tab.

Do my favorites sync across devices?

Yes! Macdio uses your personal iCloud account to keep your favorite stations, saved tracks, and settings in sync across all your Apple devices — iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Watch — automatically.

What are the system requirements?

Macdio requires iOS 18 / iPadOS 18, macOS Tahoe (macOS 26), tvOS 18, or watchOS 11 and later. It's built with 100% native SwiftUI to take full advantage of each platform.

Is my data shared with anyone?

Absolutely not. Macdio does not collect, store, or share any personal information. No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your device.

A station isn't playing. What should I do?

Some radio stations may go offline temporarily. Try refreshing the station list or check if the stream URL is still active. If it persists, contact support with the station name.

Where are my recordings saved?

Recordings are saved locally on your device. On Mac, they're in the app's documents folder; on iPhone and iPad, they're in the app's local storage. You can access them through the Recordings tab and export them at any time.
