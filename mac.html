How do I listen to internet radio on a Mac?
Get Macdio from the Mac App Store, open it, and pick from 55,000+ stations by country, genre or search. It launches at login if you want, and the menu bar player keeps it out of your way.
Is there a radio player for the menu bar?
Yes, and it is the heart of the Mac app. A click in the menu bar plays your favorite station, switches to another, or opens the compact player. No window needs to be open.
Can I record internet radio on a Mac?
Yes. One click records the live stream in high quality. Split by Track mode cuts the session into individual songs with artwork, ready to export.
Does it work with AirPlay?
Yes. Send the audio to AirPlay speakers or your TV straight from the app.
Does it sync with my iPhone?
Favorites, folders and saved songs sync through iCloud across all your Apple devices. See Macdio for iPhone and Macdio for Apple TV.
