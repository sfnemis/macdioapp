Macdio for Mac

Radio that lives in your menu bar.

No browser tabs, no websites playing in the background. Macdio is a 100% native Mac app with 55,000+ stations, one click away all day.

Yes, the Mac plays internet radio, and it does not need a browser tab to do it. Macdio is a native internet radio player for macOS with 55,000+ stations, a menu bar mini player, recording, synced lyrics and song identification straight from the stream. One purchase, no subscription, no ads.

How to listen to internet radio on your Mac

Install Macdio from the Mac App Store, open it, and pick a station by country, genre or search. Star the ones you keep coming back to and they sync to your iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Watch over iCloud. From then on the menu bar icon is enough: one click plays, switches station or opens the compact player, with no window on screen. If you prefer the full comparison of every route first, including the browser and VLC, that is in how to listen to internet radio on a Mac.

Menu bar mini player

Play, pause and switch stations from the menu bar while you work. Dynamic dock artwork shows what is on air.

Real recording

Record shows in high quality, split sessions into tagged tracks automatically, and keep everything local on your Mac.

Lyrics and Song ID

Synced lyrics follow the song live, and Macdio names the track from the stream itself with no microphone involved.

How do I listen to internet radio on a Mac?
Get Macdio from the Mac App Store, open it, and pick from 55,000+ stations by country, genre or search. It launches at login if you want, and the menu bar player keeps it out of your way. Every other route, browser, VLC and the Music app included, is compared in how to listen to internet radio on a Mac.
Is there a radio player for the menu bar?
Yes, and it is the heart of the Mac app. A click in the menu bar plays your favorite station, switches to another, or opens the compact player. No window needs to be open.
Can I record internet radio on a Mac?
Yes. One click records the live stream in high quality. Split by Track mode cuts the session into individual songs with artwork, kept in the app for playback. More in how to record internet radio, or browse all radio guides.
Is there a free internet radio app for Mac?
Macdio is a one-time purchase rather than a free app with ads or a monthly subscription, and that purchase covers every Apple device you own.
Which macOS version does it need?
macOS 26 or later, on Apple silicon and on the Intel Macs that run it.
Does it work with AirPlay?
Yes. Send the audio to AirPlay speakers or your TV straight from the app.
Does it sync with my iPhone?
Favorites, folders and saved songs sync through iCloud across all your Apple devices. See Macdio for iPhone, Macdio for iPad and Macdio for Apple TV.
