Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my own radio station?

Open the app, click the "+" button, paste any stream URL (e.g. .m3u or .pls), give it a name, and you're live in seconds.

How do I record a live stream?

While listening, click the record button. Macdio saves the audio in high quality. Find all your recordings in the Recordings tab.

Do my favorites sync across devices?

Yes! Macdio uses iCloud to keep your favorite stations and settings in sync across all your Macs automatically.

What macOS version is required?

Macdio requires macOS Tahoe (macOS 26) or later.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Macdio does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

Overview

Macdio is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

Macdio does not collect any personal information. All your data — including favorite stations, saved tracks, and recordings — stays on your device and syncs only through your personal iCloud account.

Third-Party Services

Macdio connects to the following external services solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Radio Browser API — to browse and search radio stations (no personal data is sent)
  • LRCLIB — to fetch song lyrics (no personal data is sent)
  • iTunes Search API — to fetch album artwork (no personal data is sent)

No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are used.

iCloud

If you use iCloud sync, your favorites and settings are stored in your private iCloud container. This data is managed entirely by Apple and is not accessible to us.
