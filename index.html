Song Identification

Macdio names the song
the station won't.

Thousands of stations stream music with no title, no artist, no artwork. Macdio listens in the background and identifies it for you. No microphone, no tapping. The album art simply appears.

Live Recording

Record the radio. Get back a tagged library.

Capture any live stream straight from the network, with zero ambient noise. Macdio can split the recording into one file per song, then identify the unknown ones in the background and name them for you. You started with a stream. You end with an organized music library.

Synced Lyrics

Every word, on time.

Macdio pulls time synced lyrics and scrolls them line by line as the song plays, highlighting the current line. Radio broadcasts run a little ahead, so you can nudge the timing until it lands exactly right.
