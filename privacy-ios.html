Privacy on iPhone & iPad.

How the iOS version of Macdio handles your information across iPhone, iPad, and CarPlay. Last updated May 1, 2026.

All platforms
iOS macOS tvOS watchOS

Overview

Macdio for iOS (iPhone, iPad, and CarPlay) is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. This privacy policy explains how the iOS version of the App handles your information.

Data collection

Macdio does not collect any personal information. All your data, including favorite stations, saved tracks, recordings, and preferences, stays on your device and syncs only through your personal iCloud account.

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We have no way to identify you or access your usage data.

Usage data (Privacy Nutrition Label)

As declared in the App Store Privacy Nutrition Label, Macdio collects Other Usage Data for the purpose of App Functionality. This refers to your listening history (PlaybackHistory), which stations you played, for how long, and on which device.

This data is:

  • Not linked to your identity
  • Not used for tracking
  • Stored only in your private iCloud container
  • Used solely to display your personal listening statistics across devices

Live Activities & Dynamic Island

On iPhone, Macdio displays now-playing information in the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen using Live Activities. This data (station name, song title, artwork) is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted to any server.

CarPlay

When used with CarPlay, Macdio displays station information on your vehicle's infotainment screen. No additional data is collected or transmitted during CarPlay sessions. The same privacy protections apply as the main app.

Widgets & App Groups

Macdio uses an App Group shared container (group.com.macdio.app) to share now-playing data with iOS widgets. This data stays entirely on your device and is used only to display current playback information in widgets.

Third-party services

Macdio connects to the following external services solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Radio Browser API to browse and search radio stations. No personal data is sent.
  • LRCLIB to fetch synchronized song lyrics. No personal data is sent.
  • iTunes Search API to fetch album artwork and track metadata. No personal data is sent.

iCloud sync

Your favorites, saved stations, listening history, and settings are stored in your private iCloud container. This data is managed by Apple's iCloud infrastructure and is not accessible to us.

For Apple's privacy practices, refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Audio & recordings

Recordings are stored locally on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or access your recordings.

Children's privacy

Macdio does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no personal data, the App is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@macdio.app.
