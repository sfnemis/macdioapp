Guides

Car radio, without borders.

FM gives you the stations of one city. CarPlay plus internet radio gives you every city. Setup takes less than a minute.

Setup in three steps

Install Macdio on the iPhone, connect the phone to the car (cable or wireless CarPlay), and the Macdio icon appears on the car screen. Favorites, discovery and Now Playing are laid out for glances, not reading.

Presets, like it used to be

The best car radios had six numbered buttons and zero menus. Macdio's CarPlay screen brings that back: a preset grid of your chosen stations, one tap each, big enough to hit without looking. Behind it sits the full 55,000+ station catalog when a passenger wants to explore.

Long drives, identified songs

Song ID works in the car too, from the stream itself, no microphone listening to your cabin. Heart a song at the wheel and it can land in your Apple Music Favorites automatically, ready at home.

Frequently asked questions

How much mobile data does it use?
Around 58 MB per hour at 128 kbps, so roughly 1 GB per 17 hours of listening. Many stations offer lighter streams.
Does wireless CarPlay work?
Yes, wired and wireless both, and playback recovers cleanly when the car reconnects.
What happens in a tunnel?
Streaming needs coverage; short gaps are bridged by the buffer, longer ones pause playback until the signal returns.
