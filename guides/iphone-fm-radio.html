Does the iPhone have an FM radio?

The short answer is no, and it is not coming. Here is the honest version of why, and what listeners do instead.

The short answer

There is no FM radio you can switch on in an iPhone. Apple has said publicly that its phones have no FM chip that can be enabled, and newer models also lack the antenna such a chip would need. No app can change that, because the hardware access simply is not there. Any app calling itself an FM radio for iPhone is an internet radio player.

Why people keep asking

Because plenty of Android phones did ship with a usable FM receiver, and because FM has one genuine advantage: it keeps playing when the network does not. During storms and outages, a battery radio is still the most reliable thing in the house. That argument is real, and it is worth owning a small FM radio for exactly that reason. It is not an argument about what an iPhone can do today.

What internet radio does better

Every city, not one

FM gives you the transmitters in range. Streaming gives you 55,000+ stations worldwide, including the station you grew up with in a city you left.

It knows the song

FM cannot tell you what is playing. A good streaming player names the song, shows the cover, and follows the lyrics live.

It follows you

The same stations on the phone, the Mac, the TV, the watch and the car, with favorites synced between them.

No. Apple has stated the iPhone has no FM chip that can be enabled, and modern models have no FM antenna. Radio on iPhone means internet streaming.

No. The hardware access does not exist, so no app can provide FM reception. Apps advertising it are streaming over the internet.
