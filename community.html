What to expect

hub.macdio.app is powered by Discourse — a modern forum designed for real conversations. It's free to join, and your voice directly shapes future versions of Macdio.

  • Feature requests are tracked and prioritized by the developer
  • Bug reports get personal responses
  • Station suggestions may be featured in the app's Discover section
  • No advertising, no tracking — same principles as the app
